# Innovation Brief Demo — Role-Play Guide

## Scenario
You are **Sarah Chen**, GM Consumer Cards at **Wattlebird Bank** (fictional mid-tier AU bank, ~2M cards on issue). You're being briefed by the Innovation team before a potential engagement.

## What you know

**The problem:** From 1 October 2026, the RBA is cutting the consumer credit interchange fee cap from 0.80% to 0.30%. That directly cuts the revenue that funds your rewards program. Your team estimates a **$14–18M/year shortfall** — roughly a third of your rewards budget.

**Why it's urgent:** You need changes in-market by October 1. If you just quietly cut earn rates, customers will notice, comparison sites will amplify it, and you'll cop the blame even though every bank is doing the same thing. You'd rather get ahead of it.

**Your customer base:**
- 62% of consumer credit revenue comes from rewards cards
- NPS +42 among premium cardholders
- 68% of rewards holders say points earn rate is their #1 reason for keeping the card
- Research shows dining/entertainment offers have the strongest appeal as a non-points benefit

**Your instinct:** You think a pure earn-rate cut is a death spiral — customers leave, spend drops, revenue falls further. You want to **shift the conversation from "fewer points" to "better membership"** — but you don't know exactly what that looks like yet.

**Three options on the table:**
1. **Rebalance** — modest earn-rate cut + new lifestyle benefits (dining, events, wellness)
2. **Tier restructure** — collapse 3 tiers to 2, protect premium tier earn rate, accept base-tier churn
3. **Pivot to cashback** — simpler, cheaper, but kills the emotional connection and the Frequent Flyer partnership

You're leaning toward Option A but not committed. You're open to a combination.

## How to play it

- You're time-poor and senior — you want to get to the point
- You care most about **customer retention** and **NPS protection**
- You're worried about the **Frequent Flyer co-brand** specifically — those customers are very points-elastic
- You want to know if dining/entertainment partnerships can actually work at scale
- You're sceptical of anything that sounds like "just raise the annual fee"
- If asked about budget or timeline: "We need to be in-market before October 1, and I have board approval to invest up to $5M in transition costs"

## What the tool will do

You'll be sent a link. It opens a short voice conversation (~2–3 mins) where an AI asks you about the problem in your own words. Just talk naturally as Sarah. After the call, the tool generates a brief that the consulting team uses to prepare for the engagement.

## Documents uploaded to the tool
Three PDFs have been pre-loaded as context:
1. **Wattlebird Rewards Strategy Memo** — internal strategy doc outlining the three options
2. **Cardholder Sentiment Pulse (May 2026)** — customer research summary
3. **RBA Interchange Reform Briefing** — regulatory background

The voice AI will have read these before your call.
