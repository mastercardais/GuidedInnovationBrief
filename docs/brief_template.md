# Question:
## The why:
1. Why do you want/need to run an innovation event? ​
A short summary on why you want to run a Labs’ event. What is the background to the engagement, what is the business/customer need, what is the hoped for outcome from the event? ​

2. What have you observed/learnt to decide that there is a problem? ​
The source could be industry trends, internal data analysis, some key market insights or competitor analysis. Please provide links to the relevant decks that support your assertion. 

3. Why is this a problem for your business? ​
How does the issue that you have observed impact your customer and/or your business? Please provide quantitative proof if possible. 


## Customer:
1. Who is the customer? Describe the customers that are being affected by this problem that you want to help. ​
Detail the attributes of your sweet spot customer? Why are they your target segment? How do you distinguish this segment from other customer segments? Please provide background market/customer research as needed which support your statements. ​

2. If this problem was solved why would it be good for this customer? ​
What is the key benefit(s) of your proposed solution? ​

3. What are this customer segment’s fundamental needs? ​
Example: they want mobile access, they want transparency? How do you know that these are the needs (provide any supporting research)? It is often useful to consider the customer’s functional, emotional & social motivators. ​ 

## Business information:
1. If this problem was solved why would it be good for your business? ​
What is the market opportunity for the business if this problem is solved? ​
You might address questions such as... ​
* What type of competitive advantage (if any) would your business derive if this problem was solved? ​
* Will you acquire new customers? How? ​
* Will it create a new revenue source? How? ​
* Will it positively boost the company brand? ​
* Are there any process efficiencies to be gained? 

2. Do you have an existing service/product that is currently trying to tackle the problem? ​
If so, why do you think it is not solving the problem adequately? What do customers like/dislike about this current offering? Don’t forget to capture what is working in your current offering! As always it’s useful to provide any supporting research on what works/doesn’t work with the existing solution. 

3. Is there an existing solution/offering you ADMIRE that is ​
attempting to solve the problem? ​
How are your local competitors tackling this problem? Have you seen/heard of anyone in a different industry/geography that is tackling this problem well? Do you have any competitor/market research in this area? It’s ok just to share examples of services you’ve liked with explanations on what you liked/disliked. 


## Innovation challenge statement
1. In your own words and in no more than two sentences: Describe the problem statement that you wish to solve for and why? ​
Sample HOW MIGHT WE (HMW) statements: ​
* How might we deliver a different way of paying for credit card purchases through offering an instalment based flexible repayment option enabling users the flexibility of using a card with the security of loan ​
* How might we create a financial prepaid product aimed at children which is linked to their parent’s account through an app and which teaches the child good money management habits in an engaging manner?
